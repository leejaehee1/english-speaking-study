### 챕터 11 · Language Learning Tips · 매일의 꾸준함과 영어 학습 비법 (28:28–31:55)

**David**: **This part is very special. We want to talk directly to you, the listener. Learning a new language is not easy. It takes time, patience, and practice. But you are doing it every day!**

**Alice**: **When you think about language learning, David, what is the most important thing?**

**David**: **Consistency! Consistency means doing something regularly every day—even for just 15 minutes. 15 minutes every day is much better than 3 hours on Sunday. Consistency creates progress.**

**Alice**: **And for me, the most important thing is: Don't be afraid of making mistakes! The people who improve the fastest are not the people who never make mistakes. They are the people who are not afraid to make mistakes.**

**David**: **Even when your grammar feels shaky—speak anyway!**

**Alice**: **Here are four great ways to practice English every day:**
1. **Listen every day:** Podcasts, videos, songs.
2. **Talk to yourself in English:** Narrate your day while cooking, driving, or in the shower! ("Now I am making coffee. I am adding sugar. It smells delicious.")
3. **Read English:** Start with simple texts and children's books.
4. **Write English:** Keep a simple 5-sentence daily diary!

**David**: **Remember these motivational phrases:**
- **Practice makes perfect.**
- **Every day is a chance to improve.**
- **Don't be afraid to make mistakes.**
- **Small steps lead to big progress.**
- **You are doing great. Keep going!**

### 챕터 13 · Phrasal Verbs & Conclusion · 필수 구동사 8선과 마무리 (35:10–39:49)

**David**: **Before we finish, let's talk about something essential in daily English: phrasal verbs!**

**Alice**: **Let's master the top 8 everyday phrasal verbs:**

1. **wake up** — 잠에서 깨다 (눈을 뜨다)
   - **I wake up at 7:00 every day.**
2. **get up** — 침대에서 몸을 일으켜 일어나다
   - **I wake up at 7:00, but I don't get up until 7:15.**
3. **turn on / turn off** — 기계나 불을 켜다 / 끄다
   - **I turn on the TV. I turn off the lights before bed.**
4. **put on / take off** — 옷이나 신발을 입다 / 벗다
   - **I put on my coat before leaving. I take off my shoes at home.**
5. **look for** — 무언가를 찾다
   - **I'm always looking for my keys!**
6. **find out** — 새로운 정보를 알아내다, 발견하다
   - **I found out that the restaurant was closed today.**
7. **run out of** — ~이 다 떨어지다, 바닥나다
   - **We ran out of coffee this morning—it was a disaster!**
8. **give up** — 포기하다 (절대 영어 공부를 포기하지 마세요!)
   - **Never give up on your English learning!**

**David**: **Congratulations on listening to this whole video! Think of every English sentence you practice as a workout for your brain.**

**Alice**: **Keep practicing, keep listening, and keep going. Your English is getting better every single day. Believe it! Goodbye for now!**
