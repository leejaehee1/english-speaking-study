## 대화 대본 (Script)

### 챕터 8 · Hobbies and Free Time · 여가와 취미 생활 (20:29–23:10)

**David**: **Let's talk about something fun now: hobbies! What do you enjoy doing in your free time, Alice?**

**Alice**: **Oh, I have many hobbies. I love reading books, I love cooking, and I like painting.**

**David**: **Painting? That's interesting! What do you paint?**

**Alice**: **Usually landscapes—nature scenes, mountains, rivers, forests.**

**David**: **That sounds beautiful. Are you good at painting?**

**Alice**: **Hmm, I think I am okay. Not amazing, but I enjoy it.**

**David**: **That's what matters! You do it because you love it, not because you are perfect.**

**Alice**: **Exactly. Art doesn't have to be perfect; you just enjoy the process! And what about cooking? What do you like to cook?**

**David**: **I like to cook pasta, Italian food. And also, I enjoy baking cakes and cookies.**

**Alice**: **Oh, you bake? Baking is quite difficult. You need to be very precise.**

**David**: **Yes, baking is like science. You need exact amounts. You can't just add a little bit of this and a little bit of that like in regular cooking! But when the cake comes out perfectly, it feels amazing.**

**Alice**: **I understand that satisfaction! And what other hobbies do you have?**

**David**: **I love music. I play the guitar.**

**Alice**: **Oh, wow, that's so cool! How long have you been playing?**

**David**: **I have been playing for about five years. I'm not a professional, but I can play some songs.**

**Alice**: **Can you sing, too?**

**David**: **No, definitely not! I can play guitar, but singing—let's say my neighbors don't appreciate it!**

**Alice**: **Poor neighbors! I also enjoy watching documentaries. I love learning about history, nature, and science.**

**David**: **Watching English documentaries is a great way to practice listening. You hear natural, clear English, real vocabulary, and authentic pronunciation.**

**Alice**: **Let's practice some useful phrases about hobbies:**
- **In my free time, I like to read.**
- **My hobby is painting.**
- **I enjoy cooking in my spare time.**
- **I have been playing guitar for five years.**

### 챕터 9 · Making Plans with Friends · 친구와 주말 약속 잡기 (23:13–25:06)

**David**: **Let's practice something very practical now: making plans. This is something we do all the time.**

**Alice**: **Yes, planning to meet friends or making appointments. Let's have a conversation like two friends making plans for the weekend.**

**David**: **Great, I'll start. Hey, Alice, are you free this Saturday?**

**Alice**: **Saturday? Let me think... In the morning I have something, but the afternoon should be free. Why, what are you thinking?**

**David**: **I was thinking we could go to that new coffee shop downtown—the one that opened last week.**

**Alice**: **Oh, I heard about that place! It looks really nice. They serve specialty coffee, right?**

**David**: **Yes, and they have homemade cakes: cheesecake, lemon cake...**

**Alice**: **Now you're speaking my language! Let's go!**

**David**: **Perfect. So, Saturday afternoon, maybe around 2:00?**

**Alice**: **2:00 works for me. Should we walk or take the bus?**

**David**: **It's about 20 minutes on foot or 10 minutes by bus.**

**Alice**: **Let's walk. The weather should be nice on Saturday.**

**David**: **Sounds great. Let's meet at the park entrance. We can walk together from there.**

**Alice**: **Perfect. I'll be there at 2:00. Don't be late!**

**David**: **Me, late? Never! You are always five minutes late, Alice.**

**Alice**: **Okay, maybe five minutes, but I always arrive eventually! I'll be there. See you Saturday.**

**David**: **See you then! Let's highlight the key phrases:**
- **Are you free on Saturday?** (시간 있는지 물어보기)
- **I was thinking we could go to a coffee shop.** (자연스러운 제안)
- **That works for me.** (그 시간/장소 좋아, 동의하기)
- **Should we walk or take the bus?** (이동 수단 선택)
- **Let's meet at 2:00.** (약속 시간/장소 확정)
- **See you then!** (약속 확정 후 인사)

### 챕터 10 · Travel Experiences · 여행 추억과 필수 여행 어휘 (25:11–28:17)

**David**: **Okay, now let's talk about something most people love: travel!**

**Alice**: **Oh, yes! I love traveling. Where is your favorite place you have ever visited, David?**

**David**: **That is a hard question. I think Italy. I visited Italy two years ago. Rome and Florence. It was absolutely beautiful!**

**Alice**: **Rome is so historic! The Colosseum, the Vatican, the Trevi Fountain... Did you throw a coin in the Trevi Fountain?**

**David**: **Of course! They say if you throw a coin in the fountain, you will return to Rome.**

**Alice**: **And what about Florence?**

**David**: **Florence was amazing: the art, the architecture, and the food! Real Italian pizza, authentic pasta, and gelato.**

**Alice**: **Gelato—Italian ice cream. The best ice cream in the world! And where have you traveled, Alice?**

**Alice**: **I traveled to Japan a few years ago. Tokyo. I was completely amazed! The culture, the people, the technology, and the sushi and ramen—everything was fascinating.**

**David**: **Food is such an important part of traveling. You experience the culture through the food.**

**Alice**: **Let's review essential travel vocabulary:**
- **destination** — 여행 목적지
- **accommodation** — 숙소 (hotel, hostel, Airbnb)
- **sightseeing** — 명소 관광
- **local food** — 현지 로컬 음식
- **souvenir** — 여행 기념품
- **jet lag** — 시차 증후군 (시차로 인한 피로)
- **Where are you going on vacation?**
- **I want to try the local food.**

## 🎯 Part 3 핵심 청크 (Chunks) 5선

1. **enjoy the process** — 결과보다 과정 자체를 즐기다
2. **Now you're speaking my language!** — 이제야 내 취향을 제대로 짚었네! (아주 마음에 들 때)
3. **That works for me.** — 나 그 시간/제안 괜찮아, 좋아.
4. **experience the culture through the food** — 음식을 통해 문화를 경험하다
5. **jet lag is real** — 시차 적응은 정말 만만치 않다
