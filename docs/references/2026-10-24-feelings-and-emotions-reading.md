## 전체 녹음 대본

**David:** Alice, how are you feeling right now? Really, how are you feeling right now?

**Alice:** Actually, I feel very good. Talking with you is always nice and I enjoy these conversations.

Me too. I feel happy and relaxed right now.

That's wonderful. You know, in English we have many words for feelings, for emotions.

Yes. And many learners know happy and sad, but there are so many more words. Let's explore some.

Excited. E X C I T E D. Excited means you feel very happy and full of energy about something. I am so excited about my trip.

Nervous. N E R V O U S. Nervous means you feel worried or a little scared about something, often before something important. I am nervous before my presentation.

Proud. P R O U D. Proud means you feel very good about something you did or something someone you love did. I am proud of my hard work.

Frustrated. F R U S T R A T E D. Frustrated means you feel annoyed because something is difficult or not working. I feel frustrated when I can't understand English.

This is a very common feeling for language learners.

Very common, but it's normal. Everyone feels frustrated when learning something new.

Relieved. R E L I E V E D. Relieved means you feel happy because something difficult or worrying is now over. I feel relieved that the exam is finished.

That's a great one. We all know that feeling after an exam. And one more.

Grateful. G R A T E F U L. Grateful means you feel thankful. You appreciate something or someone. I am grateful for my health. I am grateful for my family.

Grateful is a beautiful word. And feeling grateful is very good for your mental health. Studies show that people who feel grateful are happier. They worry less. They are more positive.

So, it's good to think about what you are grateful for. Every day you can think of three things you are grateful for. It doesn't have to be big things. I am grateful for this cup of coffee. I am grateful for the sunshine today. I am grateful for my comfortable bed. Small things, simple things, but they bring happiness.

That's beautiful, Alice.

Thank you. I read a lot about this topic.

I can tell. Okay, let's now practice using these emotion words in sentences. I'll say a situation and you say how you feel.

Okay, I'm ready.

You studied very hard for an English test and you got a very high score. How do you feel?

I feel proud and excited. I'm so proud of myself. I worked very hard and I did well.

Perfect. Next, you are going to speak English with someone new for the first time. How do you feel?

A little nervous, but I'm also excited. It's a good opportunity.

Great. See, you can be nervous and excited at the same time. Feelings are complex.

Yes. Often we feel more than one thing at the same time. That is completely normal.

Okay, one more. You tried very hard to do something, but it didn't work. You had to do it again. How do you feel?

Frustrated. I feel a bit frustrated, but I'm not going to give up. I'll try again.

Excellent. And that attitude, I'll try again. That is the key to success in English, in life, in everything.

Never give up. That's three simple words, but very powerful. Never give up.

Say it with us. Never give up.

Never give up.

One more time. Never give up.

## 핵심 표현·한국어 뜻

- **How are you feeling right now?** — 지금 기분이나 몸 상태가 어떤가요?
- **excited / be excited about** — 신이 난, 기대되는 / ~에 신이 나다, ~이 기대되다.
- **nervous** — 긴장한, 불안한.
- **proud / be proud of** — 자랑스러운, 뿌듯한 / ~을 자랑스럽게 여기다.
- **frustrated** — 뜻대로 되지 않아 답답한, 좌절감을 느끼는.
- **relieved** — 걱정이나 어려운 일이 끝나 안도하는, 마음이 놓인.
- **grateful / be grateful for** — 감사하는 / ~에 감사하다.
- **I feel relieved that the exam is finished.** — 시험이 끝나서 마음이 놓여요.
- **I'm so proud of myself.** — 제 자신이 정말 자랑스러워요.
- **I can tell.** — 알겠어요, 티가 나요. 이 문맥에서는 상대가 그 주제에 대해 많이 읽었다는 것이 느껴진다는 뜻입니다.
- **at the same time** — 동시에.
- **I feel a bit frustrated, but I'm not going to give up.** — 조금 답답하지만 포기하지 않을 거예요.
- **I'll try again.** — 다시 해 볼게요.
- **the key to success** — 성공의 열쇠, 성공의 핵심.
- **Never give up.** — 절대 포기하지 마세요.

## 짧게 말하기 연습

- **I feel relieved because ...** — ~해서 마음이 놓여요.
- **I'm proud of ...** — ~이 자랑스러워요.
- **I'm grateful for ...** — ~에 감사해요.
- **I'm nervous, but I'm also excited.** — 긴장되지만 기대도 돼요.
