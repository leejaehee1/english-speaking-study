## 전체 녹음 대본

Now we have a very important section. Practice questions.

These are questions for you, the listener. Think about each one. Answer in your head or say it out loud.

Saying it out loud is better. Speaking practice is so important.

Okay. Question one. What time do you wake up every morning?

Think about it and say, "I wake up at." Say the time out loud.

Good. Question two. What is your favorite food and why do you like it? Try to say two or three sentences.

My favorite food is ... I like it because ...

Excellent. Question three. If you could travel anywhere right now, where would you go?

If I could go anywhere, I would go to ... I want to go there because ...

Beautiful. Question four. What is one habit you want to change or improve?

I want to ... I think this will help me because ...

Great work. Question five. How do you feel about your English right now?

Honestly, there are no wrong answers. Be honest with yourself.

I feel blank about my English. I think I am good at blank, but I want to improve my blank.

Well done. These are not easy questions. You did great.

Every time you answer a question in English, your brain practices, your English grows.

Even if no one hears you, speaking out loud is very powerful.

Talk to yourself in English. In the shower, in the car, in the kitchen.

Talk to yourself. People might think you are a little crazy, but you will have very good English.

Worth it.

## 핵심 표현·한국어 뜻

- **answer in your head / say it out loud** — 머릿속으로 답하다 / 소리 내어 말하다.
- **What time do you wake up every morning?** — 매일 아침 몇 시에 일어나나요?
- **If you could travel anywhere right now, where would you go?** — 지금 당장 어디든 여행할 수 있다면 어디로 가고 싶나요? (가정법 과거: `If I could go anywhere, I would go to ...`)
- **What is one habit you want to change or improve?** — 바꾸거나 개선하고 싶은 습관 하나는 무엇인가요?
- **How do you feel about your English right now?** — 지금 본인의 영어에 대해 어떻게 느끼나요?
- **Be honest with yourself.** — 자기 자신에게 솔직해지세요.
- **I feel [blank] about my English.** — 빈칸(`blank`)이라는 단어를 그대로 읽어 문장 틀을 알려 주는 표현입니다.
- **Talk to yourself in English.** — 영어로 혼잣말을 해 보세요.
- **Worth it.** (`It's worth it.`) — 그럴 만한 가치가 있어요.

## 5개 질문에 내 상황으로 답해 보기

대본을 읽은 뒤 아래 5개 질문에 실제 내 이야기를 넣어 소리 내어 말해 보세요.

1. **Question 1 (기상 시간):**  
   **I usually wake up at 7 a.m.**
2. **Question 2 (좋아하는 음식과 이유):**  
   **My favorite food is Korean food. I like it because it is healthy and flavorful.**
3. **Question 3 (지금 가고 싶은 여행지와 이유):**  
   **If I could go anywhere, I would go to Vancouver. I want to go there because I want to use English in daily life.**
4. **Question 4 (바꾸거나 기르고 싶은 습관):**  
   **I want to speak English out loud every day. I think this will help me because English is like a muscle.**
5. **Question 5 (지금 내 영어에 대한 생각):**  
   **I feel excited about my English. I think I am good at studying consistently, but I want to improve my conversational vocabulary.**
