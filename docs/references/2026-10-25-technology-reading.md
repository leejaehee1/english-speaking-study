## 전체 녹음 대본

**Alice:** David, do you think technology has made our lives better or worse?

**David:** Ooh, big question. I think both.

Both. Explain.

Technology has made many things better. Communication is so much easier now. You can call or video chat with someone on the other side of the world.

Yes. 30 years ago, you had to write a letter and wait weeks for a reply. Now you send a message and get a reply in seconds.

That's amazing when you think about it. And for learning English, technology is incredible. You have YouTube, you have apps, you have podcasts.

Like us right now. You are using technology to learn English.

Exactly. Technology makes English learning more accessible. More people can learn anywhere in the world.

That's a beautiful thing. But you said technology can also be worse.

Yes. Sometimes technology creates problems too. People spend too much time on their phones. Too much social media, too many videos, and people compare themselves to others online. They see perfect pictures, perfect lives, and they feel bad about their own life.

That's a real problem. Social media sometimes makes people feel less happy. Also, technology can make us less patient. We want everything immediately. Fast internet, fast answers, fast food delivery.

Fast food delivery. Again, it keeps coming up, but it's true. We want instant results and learning English is not instant.

Not at all. Learning English takes time, months, years. But technology can make the journey more enjoyable.

Yes, use technology wisely. Don't let it control you. You control it.

Don't let technology control you. You control technology. Good phrase. Okay, let's talk about some technology vocabulary.

Yes, some modern words that are useful to know.

First, download. D O W N L O A D. To download means to get files or apps from the internet onto your device. Download the app on your phone.

Upload. U P L O A D. To upload means to send files from your device to the internet. Upload the photo to social media.

Wi-Fi. W I F I. Wi-Fi is wireless internet connection. Is there Wi-Fi here? Very important question when you travel.

The most important question.

Password. P A S S W O R D. Your password is the secret word you use to protect your accounts. What is the Wi-Fi password?

And update. U P D A T E. To update means to get the newest version of something. Update your phone. This app needs an update.

Great. Download. Upload. Wi-Fi. Password. Update. All very useful in modern life.

And one useful phrase, my internet is not working.

The most frustrating sentence in modern life. My internet is not working. Can you help me? Or simply, the Wi-Fi is slow today.

We all know that feeling. Waiting for a page to load. Waiting for a video to buffer. Patience. Technology teaches us patience. Or the opposite.

## 핵심 표현·한국어 뜻

- **Has technology made our lives better or worse?** — 기술이 우리 삶을 더 좋게 만들었을까요, 아니면 더 나쁘게 만들었을까요?
- **on the other side of the world** — 지구 반대편에 있는.
- **get a reply in seconds** — 몇 초 만에 답장을 받다.
- **make English learning more accessible** — 영어 학습을 더 쉽게 접할 수 있게 만들다.
- **compare themselves to others online** — 온라인에서 자신을 다른 사람들과 비교하다.
- **It keeps coming up.** — 그 얘기가 자꾸 나오네요. (앞선 대화에서 배달 음식 이야기가 반복해서 등장한 것을 가리킵니다.)
- **instant results** — 즉각적인 결과.
- **use technology wisely** — 기술을 현명하게 사용하다.
- **Don't let technology control you. You control technology.** — 기술이 당신을 지배하게 두지 마세요. 당신이 기술을 통제하세요.
- **download / upload** — (인터넷에서 기기로) 내려받다 / (기기에서 인터넷으로) 올리다.
- **Is there Wi-Fi here? / What is the Wi-Fi password?** — 여기 와이파이 되나요? / 와이파이 비밀번호가 뭔가요?
- **This app needs an update.** — 이 앱은 업데이트가 필요해요.
- **My internet is not working.** — 인터넷이 안 돼요.
- **The Wi-Fi is slow today.** — 오늘 와이파이가 느려요.
- **waiting for a page to load / waiting for a video to buffer** — 페이지가 열리기를 기다리는 것 / 영상이 버퍼링되기를 기다리는 것.

## 짧게 말하기 연습

- **Technology makes ... much easier.** — 기술 덕분에 …이 훨씬 쉬워졌어요.
- **I spend too much time on ...** — 저는 …에 시간을 너무 많이 써요.
- **Don't let ... control you.** — …이 당신을 휘두르게 두지 마세요.
- **My internet is not working. Can you help me?** — 인터넷이 안 되는데 도와주실 수 있나요?
