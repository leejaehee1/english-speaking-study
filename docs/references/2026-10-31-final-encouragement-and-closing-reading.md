## 전체 녹음 대본

**David:** Alice, this was such a wonderful conversation today.

**Alice:** It really was. I loved every part of it. And I want to say something to everyone listening.

What's that?

You chose to spend time learning. You could have done anything else, but you chose English. And that choice matters.

Every time you choose to listen, to practice, to try, you are choosing to grow.

Learning a language is not easy. It takes time, it takes effort, and sometimes it's frustrating.

But every minute you spend with English, it's never wasted. It all adds up.

Today's listening practice, it helped. Even if you don't notice it right now, your brain is working. It is processing everything you heard.

And next time you hear these words or phrases in real life, you will recognize them. You will understand.

That's the moment that makes it all worth it. "Oh, I know that word. I know that phrase."

And it will happen. It will happen if you keep going. So, please keep going.

Come back tomorrow or the day after. Watch another video. Listen more.

Each video is more practice, more progress, more confidence.

And remember, you are not alone. There are learners all over the world just like you. All trying, all growing, all listening to English conversations in their kitchens, their cars, their rooms.

You are part of something big, a global community of English learners, and we are so happy you are part of ours.

Before we go, we have one question for you. We love reading your answers in the comments.

Here is today's question. What topic do you want us to talk about in our next video? Tell us in the comments.

Any topic, work, family, travel, sports, health, culture, anything. We read every comment and your ideas help us make better videos.

So, write your answer below. "I want you to talk about blank." We can't wait to read what you write.

Thank you so much for spending this time with us today. It was truly our pleasure.

Keep listening, keep learning, keep going, and we will see you in the next video.

Goodbye for now.

Goodbye. Take care everyone.

## 자막 교정 메모

- **I want you to talk about Blink. → I want you to talk about blank.**: 앞선 10월 29일 대본(`I feel blank about my English.`)과 마찬가지로 빈칸을 뜻하는 **blank**(`/blæŋk/`)가 자동 자막에서 **Blink**로 잘못 인식된 부분입니다. 낭독할 때는 `/æ/` 모음을 살려 **blank**로 읽습니다.

## 핵심 표현·한국어 뜻

- **You could have done anything else, but you chose English.** — 다른 어떤 일을 할 수도 있었지만, 여러분은 영어를 선택했어요. (`could have p.p.`: ~할 수도 있었다)
- **That choice matters.** — 그 선택은 중요해요(의미가 커요).
- **It takes time, it takes effort.** — 시간이 걸리고 노력이 필요해요.
- **Every minute you spend with English, it's never wasted.** — 영어와 함께 보내는 모든 순간은 절대 헛되지 않아요.
- **It all adds up.** — 그것들이 모두 차곡차곡 쌓여요(모여서 큰 결과를 만들어요).
- **That's the moment that makes it all worth it.** — 바로 그 순간이 이 모든 노력을 가치 있게 만들어 줘요.
- **tomorrow or the day after** — 내일이나 모레(그다음 날).
- **What topic do you want us to talk about?** — 우리가 어떤 주제로 이야기하기를 원하시나요?
- **We can't wait to read what you write.** — 여러분이 적어 주실 글을 빨리 읽어 보고 싶어요(무척 기대돼요).
- **It was truly our pleasure.** — 정말 큰 기쁨이었어요(함께해서 정말 즐거웠어요).
- **Goodbye for now. / Take care everyone.** — 이만 인사드릴게요. / 모두 몸 건강히 잘 지내세요.

## 짧게 말하기 연습
