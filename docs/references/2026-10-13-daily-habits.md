## 녹음 대본

사용자가 제공한 자막을 문장별로 정리했습니다. 화자는 대화 흐름에 따라 **David / Alice**로 표시했으며 실제 음성을 우선합니다. 음악·웃음 표시는 생략합니다.

학습용으로 4:50의 `when we were young`을 현재 일반적 습관을 말하는 **when we are young**으로 교정했습니다. 그 밖에는 자막의 문장 연결과 문장부호만 다듬었습니다.

::: details 대본 펼치기

**Alice:** Of course. Nobody is perfect every day.

**David:** Exactly. What about you, Alice? What habits do you have?

**Alice:** Well, I have some good habits and some bad habits.

**David:** Tell us both.

**Alice:** Okay. Good habits first. Every morning I make my bed.

**David:** Oh, really? That is a very good habit.

**Alice:** Yes. My mother taught me. She always said, “If you make your bed, your day will be organized.”

**David:** That is wise advice.

**Alice:** I didn’t believe her when I was a child, but now I do.

**David:** We never listen to our parents when we are young, and then we get older and think they were right.

**Alice:** Exactly. So true. Okay. Another good habit: I read before bed every night.

**David:** What kind of books do you read?

**Alice:** I like stories, novels. Sometimes I read in English, sometimes in my first language.

**David:** Reading in English is very good practice. For everyone listening, if you want to improve your English, try reading simple English books before bed.

**Alice:** Even just ten pages, even five pages every day.

**David:** Little by little, you improve. Slowly but surely.

**Alice:** Slowly but surely. I like that phrase.

**David:** Let’s say it together. Are you ready? Slowly…

**Alice:** …but surely.

**David:** Slowly but surely. It means little by little, step by step, you will get there.

**Alice:** And it is true for English learning. You will not become fluent in one day.

**David:** No. But if you practice a little every day…

**Alice:** …slowly but surely, your English gets better.

**David:** Beautiful. Okay, Alice, you said you have bad habits too. Tell us.

**Alice:** Oh no. Do I have to?

**David:** Yes. Tell us. We are all friends here.

**Alice:** Okay. Okay. My bad habit: I look at my phone too much.

**David:** Ah, many people have this problem.

**Alice:** I know. I wake up in the morning, and the first thing I do is check my phone.

**David:** That is a very common bad habit.

**Alice:** I check messages. I check social media. I check the news.

**David:** And before you know it, thirty minutes are gone.

**Alice:** Exactly. Thirty minutes in bed just looking at my phone.

**David:** So that is why you are late getting up.

**Alice:** Yes. The phone is the problem, not me. It is the phone’s fault.

**David:** I’m sure the phone is very sorry.

**Alice:** It should be.

**David:** Okay, listeners, do you have this habit too? Do you look at your phone first thing in the morning? Think about it. And if you do, try to change it. Try to wait. Wait thirty minutes before you look at your phone.

**Alice:** It is not easy, but it makes your morning better. I am trying, slowly but surely.

**David:** Slowly but surely.

:::

## 기억할 표현

- **make my bed** — 침대를 정리하다. **Every morning I make my bed.**
- **read before bed** — 잠들기 전에 읽다. **I read before bed every night.**
- **slowly but surely** — 느리지만 꾸준히, 확실하게.
- **before you know it** — 어느새 / 자기도 모르는 사이에.
- **first thing in the morning** — 아침에 일어나자마자 / 아침 가장 먼저.
- **The first thing I do is check my phone.** — 내가 가장 먼저 하는 일은 휴대폰을 확인하는 거예요.

## 발음과 리듬

- [5:17 · Slowly but surely](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=v07kPyPkLYw&t=317s): **slowly**, **surely**를 중심으로 리듬을 따라 합니다.
- [6:03 · Before you know it](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=v07kPyPkLYw&t=363s): 네 단어를 따로 끊기보다 한 덩어리로 듣고 말합니다.
- [6:21 · 질문의 억양](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=v07kPyPkLYw&t=381s): **Do you have this habit too?**의 억양을 듣고 자신의 습관을 묻는 질문으로 바꿉니다.

휴대폰이 잘못했고 미안해할 거라는 부분은 장난스럽게 말하는 대목입니다. 말의 뜻과 실제 의도를 구별하며 어조를 들어봅니다.

## 내 상황으로 연습

- **One good habit I have is ___.** — 내 좋은 습관 하나는 ~이에요.
- **One bad habit I have is ___.** — 내 나쁜 습관 하나는 ~이에요.

전체 듣기 → 한 문장씩 따라 하기 → 두 역할 녹음 순서로 연습하고, 끝에 내 습관 두 문장을 말합니다. 녹음 파일은 따로 보관하지 않습니다.
