**David:** Okay, let's now do something special. We are going to say important phrases slowly and clearly.

This helps your brain remember the phrases and helps you say them correctly.

I will say the phrase, Alice will repeat it, then we both say it together, and then you say it, too.

Are you ready? Let's start.

I am learning English every day.

I am learning English every day.

Together. I am learning English every day.

Your turn. Say it out loud. I am learning English every day.

Great. Next.

I am getting better and better.

I am getting better and better.

Together. I am getting better and better.

Your turn. I am getting better and better.

Excellent.

English is becoming easier for me.

English is becoming easier for me.

Together. English is becoming easier for me.

You say it. English is becoming easier for me.

I understand more English now than before.

I understand more English now than before.

Together. I understand more English now than before.

Your turn. I understand more English now than before.

I am proud of my progress.

I am proud of my progress.

That was beautiful. I hope you said that and felt it. Really felt it because it's true.

## 오늘의 6대 다짐 문장 · 한국어 뜻

- **I am learning English every day.** — 나는 매일 영어를 배우고 있어요.
- **I am getting better and better.** — 나는 점점 더 나아지고(실력이 늘고) 있어요. (`비교급 and 비교급`: 점점 더 ~해지다)
- **English is becoming easier for me.** — 영어가 나에게 점점 더 쉬워지고 있어요.
- **I understand more English now than before.** — 나는 예전보다 지금 영어를 더 많이 이해해요.
- **I am proud of my progress.** — 나는 나의 발전(성장)이 자랑스러워요.
- **I will not give up. I will keep going.** — 나는 포기하지 않을 거예요. 계속 해 나갈 거예요.

## 추가 핵심 표현

- **Say it and mean it.** — 그냥 입으로만 읽지 말고 진심을 담아 말하세요. (`mean it`: 진심으로 말하다)
- **That makes all the difference.** — 바로 그것이 결정적인(가장 큰) 차이를 만들어요.
