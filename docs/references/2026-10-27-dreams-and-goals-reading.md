## 전체 녹음 대본

**David:** Alice, what is your biggest dream?

**Alice:** My biggest dream to write a book.

A book? Really? What kind of book?

I want to write a book about language learning, about how to learn a language in a fun way.

That sounds perfect for you. You know about this topic.

Thank you. It's something I think about a lot. Maybe one day.

Why maybe? Why not definitely?

You're right. Definitely. One day I will write that book.

There. See the difference? Maybe one day I will write a book versus definitely, one day I will write that book. The language we use changes how we think about our goals.

Exactly. If you say maybe, you give yourself an excuse not to do it. But if you say I will, you commit. You make a promise to yourself.

I will. Two small but powerful words. I will improve my English. I will practice every day. I will keep going even when it's hard.

Say those sentences with us.

I will improve my English.

I will improve my English.

I will practice every day.

I will practice every day.

I will keep going even when it's hard.

I will keep going even when it's hard.

Wonderful. Those are powerful promises to yourself.

**Alice:** And what is your biggest dream, David?

**David:** My biggest dream, I want to visit every continent.

Every continent? All seven?

All seven? Europe, Asia, Africa, North America, South America, Australia, and Antarctica.

Antarctica? Really? The cold one with the penguins?

Yes, the cold one with the penguins. I want to see the ice, the wild nature. It must be incredible.

That is a big dream. Have you visited many continents already?

I've been to four so far. Still need South America, Australia, and Antarctica.

You're more than halfway there.

Yes, and I believe I will do it. I will visit all seven continents.

See, I will. Not maybe.

Exactly. I'm practicing what I preach.

Practice what you preach. Let's explain this idiom. Practice what you preach means do what you tell others to do. Don't just talk about it. Do it.

Like a fitness teacher who exercises every day. They don't just tell you to exercise, they do it themselves. They practice what they preach.

Great idiom. Use it. You should practice what you preach.

Now, let's talk about goals. Big dreams are great, but we also need smaller goals.

Yes, a big dream can feel very far away, so we break it into small steps.

Small goal one, learn 10 new words this week.

Small goal two, watch one English video every day.

Small goal three, write five English sentences in a notebook each morning.

Each small goal brings you closer to the big dream. Step by step, day by day, you get there.

Step by step. Let's say that.

Step by step.

Step by step and day by day.

Day by day.

These are great phrases. Simple, encouraging, step by step, and day by day.

**David:** Alice, we've talked about so many things today.

We have daily routines, food, travel, work, health, technology, English learning, dreams.

## 원문·낭독 메모

- **My biggest dream to write a book.**: 구어에서 `My biggest dream? To write a book.`(또는 `My biggest dream is to write a book.`)이 이어진 자동 자막입니다. 읽을 때는 `My biggest dream` 뒤에 짧게 쉬고 `to write a book`을 자연스럽게 이어 읽습니다.
- **All seven? Europe, Asia, ...**: 앞 화자의 질문(`All seven?`)을 받아 `All seven.`으로 답하며 일곱 대륙을 나열하는 부분입니다. 두 번째 `All seven`은 마침표 억양(내림조)으로 읽으면 자연스럽습니다.
- **We have daily routines, ...**: 앞의 `We've talked about so many things today.`에 대한 맞장구(`We have.`: 정말 많이 이야기했네요) 뒤에 오늘 다룬 주제들을 나열하는 문장입니다. `We have` 뒤에 살짝 끊어 읽습니다.

## 핵심 표현·한국어 뜻

- **What is your biggest dream?** — 가장 큰 꿈이 무엇인가요?
- **give yourself an excuse not to do it** — 하지 않을 핑계를 스스로에게 주다.
- **commit / make a promise to yourself** — 다짐하다(전념하다) / 자기 자신에게 약속하다.
- **I will keep going even when it's hard.** — 힘들 때에도 계속 해 나갈 거예요.
- **visit every continent** — 모든 대륙을 방문하다. (**Antarctica**: 남극 대륙, `/ænˈtɑːrk.tɪ.kə/`)
- **I've been to four so far.** — 지금까지 네 곳에 가 봤어요.
- **You're more than halfway there.** — 벌써 절반 넘게 왔네요(목표의 절반 이상을 이뤘네요).
- **Practice what you preach.** — 자신이 남에게 말한 대로 직접 실천하라(언행일치하라).
- **break it into small steps** — 그것을 작은 단계들로 나누다(쪼개다).
- **bring you closer to the big dream** — 큰 꿈에 더 가까워지게 해 주다.
- **step by step, day by day** — 한 걸음씩 차근차근, 하루하루 꾸준히.

## 짧게 말하기 연습

- **My biggest dream is to ...** — 제 가장 큰 꿈은 …하는 것이에요.
- **I will keep going even when ...** — …할 때에도 포기하지 않고 계속할 거예요.
- **I'm trying to practice what I preach.** — 제가 말한 것을 직접 실천하려고 노력하고 있어요.
- **I'll break my goal into small steps.** — 제 목표를 작은 단계들로 나누어 실천할 거예요.
