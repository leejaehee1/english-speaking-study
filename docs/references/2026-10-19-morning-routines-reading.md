## 내용 정리

기상 시간, 아침형·저녁형 생활, 일어나자마자 하는 일, 물과 커피, 아침 식사, 산책과 요가에 관해 이야기합니다. 서로의 생활 습관을 묻고 답하며 이유와 느낌을 덧붙이는 연습입니다. 건강 관련 발언은 대화 속 인물의 설명으로 읽습니다.

## 핵심 chunks와 한국어 뜻

- **I'm not a morning person at all.** — 저는 전혀 아침형 인간이 아니에요.
- **I work better at night.** — 저는 밤에 일이 더 잘돼요.
- **first thing in the morning** — 아침에 일어나자마자.
- **It's a habit.** — 습관이에요.
- **right away** — 바로, 즉시.
- **That makes sense.** — 말이 되네요. / 이해돼요.
- **What do you eat for breakfast?** — 아침으로 무엇을 드세요?
- **three times a week** — 일주일에 세 번.
- **go for a walk** — 산책하러 가다.
- **It helps me wake up.** — 잠을 깨는 데 도움이 돼요.
- **I feel less stressed.** — 스트레스를 덜 받아요.
- **A good morning makes a good day.** — 좋은 아침이 좋은 하루를 만들어요.

## 녹음 방법

1. 원본을 듣고 의미 단위로 끊어 읽습니다.
2. 아래 전체 대본을 처음부터 끝까지 이어 읽습니다. 웃음·음악 표시는 읽지 않습니다.
3. 마지막 문장이 끝난 뒤 녹음을 마칩니다. 영상 시간에 맞추려고 서두르지 않습니다.
4. 제출 후에만 완료 여부를 별도로 기록합니다. 제출 안내는 기존 방식인 자정까지 카카오톡입니다.

## 내 이야기로 바꾸기 · 선택 연습

아래는 연습용 틀이며 사용자의 실제 생활 기록이 아닙니다.

- **I usually wake up at ___.** — 저는 보통 ___시에 일어나요.
- **The first thing I do is ___.** — 제가 가장 먼저 하는 일은 ___예요.
- **I usually have ___ for breakfast.** — 저는 보통 아침으로 ___을 먹어요.
- **I ___ three times a week because it helps me ___.** — 저는 ___에 도움이 되어서 일주일에 세 번 ___해요.

## 표현 메모

- **wake up**은 잠에서 깨다, **get up**은 잠자리에서 일어나다입니다.
- **every day**는 매일, **almost every day**는 거의 매일입니다.
- **I put milk and sugar.**는 원문 발화를 보존했습니다. 내 문장으로 연습할 때는 **I put milk and sugar in my coffee.**처럼 대상을 덧붙일 수 있습니다.
- **two spoons**는 원문 그대로입니다. 설탕 양을 더 구체적으로 말하려면 **two teaspoons of sugar**(설탕 두 티스푼)를 쓸 수 있습니다.

## 연속 녹음용 전체 대본

Alice, can I ask you something personal?

Of course, David. What do you want to know?

What time do you wake up in the morning?

Ooh, that's a good question. Honestly, I wake up at 6:30 every day.

6:30 every day. Even on weekends?

Well, almost every day. On Sunday, maybe I sleep until 7, but that's my big rest day.

7 o'clock is your big rest? That's very early.

I know, I know. But I like mornings. I feel good in the morning. I feel fresh. I feel ready.

I am the opposite. I am not a morning person at all.

Really? What time do you wake up?

I try to wake up at 7, but sometimes it's more like 8 or 8:30.

David, that's late.

I know, but I work better at night. My brain wakes up in the evening.

That's interesting. People are very different. Some people are morning people. Some people are night people.

Yes. And neither one is wrong. We are all different.

Exactly. So, David, when you wake up, what do you do first?

First, I look at my phone. I know. I know that's bad.

Oh, no, David. Doctors say you should not look at your phone first thing in the morning.

I know. But it's a habit. I check my messages. I check the time. I check the weather.

And what do you see?

Usually messages from people in different time zones because when I sleep, they are working.

Yes, that happens to me too. I wake up and I have 20 messages.

Exactly. So Alice, what do you do first when you wake up?

I drink water. A big glass of water right away.

Oh, that's good. Why water?

Because your body is like a little dry after sleeping. You sleep for seven or eight hours, no water. So your body needs water in the morning.

That makes sense. Good idea.

And then I make coffee. Black coffee. No sugar.

No sugar. Wow. Strong?

Yes. I need strong coffee to start my day. What about you? Do you drink coffee?

Yes, but I put milk and sugar. A lot of sugar, actually.

A lot. How much?

Two spoons. Sometimes three.

Three spoons of sugar. David, that is so sweet.

I know. My coffee is more like dessert. It's a dessert coffee.

A dessert coffee. I love that. Okay, so water, coffee, then what?

Then I get dressed. I eat breakfast and then I start work.

What do you eat for breakfast?

Usually something simple. Bread maybe or eggs. Sometimes both.

Eggs in the morning. That's good. Protein is good for the morning.

Yes, eggs give you energy. You feel full for a long time.

I agree. I usually eat yogurt in the morning with some fruit. Simple but healthy.

That sounds nice. I should try that.

You should. Okay, let's talk about exercise. Do you exercise in the morning?

Sometimes. Not every day. Maybe three times a week.

What do you do?

I go for a walk. Just a short walk, maybe 20 or 30 minutes.

That's great. Walking is very good for you.

Yes. And it helps me wake up. When I walk, I feel more awake, more ready for the day.

I understand. I do yoga in the morning.

Yoga. Nice. Every day?

Almost every day. Just 15 or 20 minutes. Nothing very long. But it helps me feel calm, feel centered.

Calm is good, especially before a busy day.

Exactly. If I do yoga in the morning, I feel better all day. I feel less stressed.

Stress is a big problem for many people, right?

Yes. Many people feel very stressed. Work, family, money. There are many things to think about and morning routines can help.

A good morning makes a good day.

I completely agree. And now let's say that phrase together slowly.

A good morning makes a good day.

One more time. A good morning makes a good day.

Great. Remember that phrase. It's simple, but it's true.
