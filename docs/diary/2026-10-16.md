What a wonderful day! The weather was so nice!

I studied English again today, even though I wasn't feeling great because I hadn't gotten enough sleep.

After class, we had lunch together, and I enjoyed teaching the others a few things about web technology.

It was a very Korean-style meal!

Today, I also realized something important about learning English.

We've been getting plenty of input, but we haven't been practicing speaking and writing enough.

So, as soon as I got home, I watched a YouTube video about how to improve my speaking skills through practice.

Then, I spent about an hour practicing speaking along with a video that played English expressions continuously.

It was fascinating because I could feel my brain getting trained to respond in English.

Instead of analyzing every sentence, I was practicing connecting English expressions directly to their meanings.

I'm going to keep practicing hard tomorrow!
