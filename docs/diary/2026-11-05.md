## 자연스러운 영어 첨삭본

Today, I was amazed to see a Taekwondo board-breaking demonstration hosted by Kukkiwon. There were about 20 demonstration team members under the age of 30, along with around 80 ordinary citizens. They were all wearing official Taekwondo uniforms, called *dobok*. It was really impressive because they performed amazing moves, spinning their bodies quickly and jumping super high to kick and break wooden boards in midair. At the end, everyone performed the same routine together. It was so beautiful because all of their movements were perfectly in sync. I took some pictures and recorded a video, though I couldn't capture the whole performance—just a few short clips.

Later today, I got to the study room a little late and started doing today's recording homework. Just then, my study mate arrived, so I stopped recording. Actually, the recording wasn't going well anyway because I messed up near the beginning. Still, I kept reading it out loud until the end because I needed to warm up my tongue muscles.

Today was also a very meaningful day because I started my vibe coding course with my study group members. We are going to build our own websites and eventually teach others how to do it. Even though we are currently in a tough situation, a new opportunity is coming our way at the same time. So we felt really great today.

## 주요 교정

- **Today, I was suprised with showing the action that is Tae-Kwon-Do hit small something, it opend by Kook-Ki-Won. → Today, I was amazed (또는 surprised) to see a Taekwondo board-breaking demonstration hosted by Kukkiwon.** —
  1. 철자 교정: `suprised` → **surprised**(`r` 포함), `opend` → **opened**.
  2. `with showing the action`은 ‘내가 시범을 보여서’처럼 들리므로 ‘~을 보고 놀랐다/감탄했다’는 **I was surprised (또는 amazed) to see ~** 로 씁니다.
  3. 태권도 시범 공연은 **a Taekwondo demonstration** (줄여서 **demo**), 송판 등을 차서 깨는 ‘격파’는 **board breaking** (또는 **breaking wooden boards**), 국기원이 주최한 행사는 **hosted (또는 held) by Kukkiwon** 으로 표현합니다.
- **There were many players younger than 30 and about 20 people. And there also were citizen about 80 people. → There were about 20 demonstration team members under 30, along with around 80 citizens.** — 무술 시범단원은 `player`(경기 선수)보다 **demonstration team members** 또는 **performers** 가 자연스럽습니다. ‘약 ~명’은 명사 앞에 붙여 **about 20 team members**, **around 80 citizens**(복수형 `-s`)로 씁니다.
- **They put on the clothes that is formal Tae-Kwon-Do trainnig clothes. → They were all wearing official Taekwondo uniforms.** —
  1. 철자 교정: `trainnig` → **training**(`n` 1개).
  2. `put on`은 ‘옷을 입는 동작’이고, 이미 입고 있는 상태는 **were wearing** 을 씁니다. ‘정식 태권도복’은 **official Taekwondo uniforms** 라고 합니다.
- **So, it was very impressed because they did well actions that is twisting their body quickly and jumping very high with kicking some meterials. → It was really impressive because they performed amazing moves, spinning their bodies quickly and jumping very high to kick and break wooden boards.** —
  1. 공연·장면(`it`)이 감동을 주는 것이므로 `impressed` 대신 **impressive**(인상적인·멋진)를 씁니다(사람이 주어일 때 **I was very impressed**).
  2. 철자 교정: `meterials` → **materials**(`m-a-t-e-r-i-a-l-s`). 격파용 송판은 **wooden boards** 라고 하면 가장 생생합니다.
  3. 공중에서 몸을 빠르게 한 바퀴 돌리는 회전 동작은 **spinning their bodies quickly in midair** 로 표현합니다.
- **lastly they act same action together ... all of them actions is same perfectly. → At the end, they performed the same routine together ... all of their movements were perfectly in sync.** — 여러 사람의 동작이 칼군무처럼 똑같이 맞아떨어질 때 원어민이 가장 즐겨 쓰는 표현은 **perfectly in sync**(`/ɪn sɪŋk/`, 호흡·동작이 완벽하게 일치하는)입니다.
- **I took a picture and record the video. But I couldt' record all I could record some small actions. → I took some pictures and recorded a video, though I couldn't capture the whole performance—just a few short clips.** —
  1. 과거 시제 일치: `took`에 맞춰 **recorded** 로 씁니다.
  2. 축약형 철자: `couldt'` → **couldn't**.
  3. 짧은 영상 조각들은 `small actions` 대신 **a few short clips** 라고 하면 아주 자연스럽습니다.
- **went to study room little lately ... failed around starting point. So, I just spoke at the end, because I need to starting my tongue's muscle. → got to the study room a little late ... messed up near the beginning. Still, I kept reading it out loud until the end because I needed to warm up my tongue muscles.** —
  1. **late vs. lately 구분**: ‘늦게’는 **late** 이고, **lately** 는 ‘최근에(요즘)’라는 뜻입니다(**a little late**: 조금 늦게).
  2. 시작 부분에서 실수해 꼬였다는 말은 **messed up (또는 made a mistake) near the beginning** 이라고 합니다.
  3. `need to` 뒤에는 동사원형이 오며, 말하기 전에 ‘혀 근육을 풀다(입을 풀다)’는 준비운동처럼 **warm up my tongue muscles**(또는 **loosen up my tongue**)라고 하면 완벽합니다.
- **We will make own's website ... Currently, we placed with difficult atmosphere, and concurrently the oppotunity was comming. → We will build our own websites ... Even though we are currently in a tough situation, a new opportunity is coming our way at the same time.** —
  1. ‘우리만의 웹사이트를 만들다’는 **build our own websites** 로 씁니다(`own` 뒤에는 `'s`를 붙이지 않고 `소유격 + own + 명사` 어순으로 씁니다).
  2. 철자 교정: `oppotunity` → **opportunity**(`r` 포함), `comming` → **coming**(`m` 1개).
  3. ‘어려운 상황에 놓여 있다’는 **we are in a tough (또는 difficult) situation** 으로 표현합니다.

- **a Taekwondo board-breaking demonstration hosted by Kukkiwon** — 국기원이 주최한 태권도 격파 시범 공연.
- **wear official Taekwondo uniforms** — 정식 태권도복(도복)을 입다.

- **All of their movements were perfectly in sync.** — 그들의 모든 동작이 칼군무처럼 완벽하게 일치했다.

- **warm up my tongue muscles** — 혀 근육을 풀다(입을 풀다).
- **build our own websites** — 우리만의 웹사이트를 만들다.
- **A new opportunity is coming our way.** — 우리에게 새로운 기회가 찾아오고 있다.
