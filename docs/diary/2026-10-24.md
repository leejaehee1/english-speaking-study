## 자연스러운 영어 첨삭본

Today has been a good day because my exam and the course I was teaching are both over.

I was actually quite nervous about having to prepare for the exam and teach at the same time, with so little time available. I was unexpectedly asked to help teach the course, so I couldn't spend as much time studying for the exam as I wanted.

Fortunately, both the exam and the course went very well. The exam was easier than I had expected, and it covered material I had studied. I think I'll get the score I was hoping for.

The course also ended well, and the final project presentations were excellent. Even though the students had only two days left, they worked hard and showed a great attitude.

Now I finally have time to relax, both physically and mentally. I also went to the gym today and did leg presses and other leg exercises.

I'm happy that I've lost weight. I hadn't had any alcohol for two weeks because I was busy with the exam and teaching, and I think that helped me lose weight.

Many students thanked me and said they had learned a lot. I felt very grateful for their positive attitude and for the effort they put into the course. I was also glad that they understood what I taught. If they hadn't understood my lessons, I wouldn't have felt as happy.

## 주요 교정

- **Today is good day → Today has been a good day.** — **a good day**처럼 관사가 필요합니다. 오늘 지금까지를 돌아보는 일기라 현재완료도 자연스럽습니다.
- **I finished the exam and teaching course → my exam and the course I was teaching are both over** — 시험과 내가 가르치던 과정이 모두 끝났다는 뜻을 분명히 했습니다. **teaching course**만 쓰면 교사 양성 과정으로 읽힐 수 있습니다.
- **the exam and teaching not enough time at the same time → having to prepare for the exam and teach at the same time, with so little time available** — 시험 준비와 강의를 병행하느라 시간이 부족했다는 뜻입니다.
- **I called to support teaching → I was unexpectedly asked to help teach the course.** — 내가 연락한 것이 아니라 강의를 도와 달라는 요청을 받은 것이므로 수동태로 옮겼습니다.
- **I didn't study more → I couldn't spend as much time studying ... as I wanted** — 원하는 만큼 공부할 시간을 내지 못했다는 뜻입니다.
- **are finished very good → went very well** — 시험과 강의가 잘 진행됐다는 뜻입니다. 동사를 꾸밀 때는 형용사 **good** 대신 부사 **well**을 씁니다.
- **easy more than I thought → easier than I had expected** — 예상보다 쉬웠다는 비교에는 **easier than**을 씁니다.
- **it consisted I studied pages → it covered material I had studied** — 공부한 내용이 시험에 나왔다는 뜻입니다. **cover material**은 내용을 다루다입니다. **consist of**를 쓰려면 **It consisted of questions on material I had studied.**처럼 쓸 수 있습니다.
- **I can expect my expected score → I think I'll get the score I was hoping for.** — 기대하던 점수를 받을 것 같다는 뜻입니다. 아직 점수가 나온 것으로 표현하지 않았습니다.
- **final project's presentation is so good → the final project presentations were excellent** — 여러 학생·팀의 발표로 해석해 복수형을 썼고, 끝난 발표이므로 과거형을 사용했습니다.
- **Students have worked hard and very good attitude despite just 2 days left → Even though the students had only two days left, they worked hard and showed a great attitude.** — **work hard / show a great attitude**로 동사 구조를 갖췄습니다. **despite** 뒤에는 **despite having only two days left**처럼 명사나 동명사가 필요합니다.
- **relaxed time physical and mental both → time to relax, both physically and mentally** — 몸과 마음 모두 쉴 시간이라는 뜻입니다. **physically / mentally**는 **relax**를 꾸미는 부사입니다.
- **went to gym / worked out leg press → went to the gym / did leg presses** — **go to the gym**이 자연스럽습니다. 운동 종목을 말할 때는 **do leg presses / do squats**처럼 표현합니다.
- **I am very happy why I loose my weight → I'm happy that I've lost weight.** — 기쁜 이유나 내용을 **happy that + 문장**으로 연결합니다. 체중이 줄었다는 **lose weight**, 그 결과가 현재까지 이어지면 **have lost weight**입니다.
- **loose / alchole → lose / alcohol** — **lose**는 잃다·줄다, **loose**는 느슨한입니다. **lose–lost–lost**의 변화도 함께 익히세요.
- **I didn't drink ... for 2 weeks by the exam and teaching → I hadn't had any alcohol for two weeks because I was busy with the exam and teaching** — 시험과 강의를 마친 시점까지 2주 동안 술을 마시지 않았다는 뜻으로 과거완료를 썼습니다.
- **many students told me thank very much → Many students thanked me.** — **thank + 사람**은 ~에게 감사하다입니다. 직접 인용하면 **Many students said, "Thank you so much."**로 쓸 수 있습니다.
- **and I could learn a lot → and said they had learned a lot** — 학생들이 ‘많이 배웠다’고 말했다는 의미로 해석했습니다. 아래 해석 메모를 참고하세요.
- **Because they attend ... very good attitude → I felt very grateful for their positive attitude ...** — 독립된 **Because** 문장을 앞 문장과 연결하고 **grateful for + 감사한 대상**으로 표현했습니다.
- **they understood my teaching → they understood what I taught** — 내가 가르친 내용을 이해했다는 뜻입니다.
- **If they didn't understand ... I didn't be happy → If they hadn't understood my lessons, I wouldn't have felt as happy.** — 이미 끝난 수업과 다른 상황을 가정하므로 **If + had + 과거분사, would have + 과거분사**를 사용했습니다. ‘그만큼 기쁘지는 않았을 것’으로 자연스럽게 표현했습니다.

## 의미 해석 메모

- 시험과 강의의 완료 날짜를 오늘로 단정하지 않고, 오늘은 둘 다 끝난 상태여서 기분이 좋다는 뜻으로 옮겼습니다. 전날 일기와도 이어지는 표현입니다.
- **I called to support teaching**는 앞선 일기의 맥락에 따라 갑자기 강의를 도와 달라는 요청을 받았다는 뜻으로 해석했습니다.
- **it consisted I studied pages**는 공부했던 자료의 내용이 시험에 나왔다는 뜻으로 해석했습니다. 특정 페이지 번호는 덧붙이지 않았습니다.
- **I could learn a lot**는 앞의 **many students told me**와 연결해 학생들이 많이 배웠다고 말했다는 뜻으로 해석했습니다. 본인이 학생들에게 배운 점이 많았다는 뜻이라면 **Many students thanked me, and I also learned a lot from them.**으로 바꾸면 됩니다.
- 체중 감소와 금주의 관계는 사용자의 경험에 대한 생각으로 표현하기 위해 **I think that helped me lose weight**로 옮겼습니다.
- 금주가 지금도 계속된다는 점을 강조하려면 **I haven't had any alcohol for two weeks.**로 쓸 수 있습니다.
- 마지막 문장은 끝난 수업에 대한 가정으로 해석했습니다. 평소의 일반적인 마음이라면 **If my students don't understand what I teach, I don't feel happy.**로 표현할 수 있습니다.
