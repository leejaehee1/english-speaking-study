# 10월 13일 · 비 오는 날과 새 스터디룸

**일기 작성·첨삭·공책 필사 완료 · 2026-10-13**

## 교정한 일기

The weather was really bad today. It started raining early in the morning, so I didn’t walk to the study room because I thought my shoes would get wet.

I really hate getting my shoes wet, so I had to take the bus. I got off after just two stops, which felt like a waste. It was only two stops away! Honestly, I’m trying to lose weight, so walking is good for me. But the bad weather kept me from walking this morning.

Today, we went to a new study room that was really nice. First of all, it was free. Second, it had a nice TV and a whiteboard. Lastly, it was very quiet, even though there were people dancing in the room next door. We couldn’t hear them at all.

Unfortunately, it was hard to book the room for another day, so we had to look for another place.

Eventually, I found another place we could use for free. However, it wasn’t easy to get to by public transportation, and we had to use it an hour earlier than before.

There are seven people in our group, but the room only had six seats. Luckily, one person couldn’t make it today, so there were enough seats for everyone.

The room also had a TV, a comfortable table, and comfortable chairs.

### 의미 확인이 필요한 부분

원문의 시간 표현은 “새 장소를 이전보다 한 시간 일찍 이용해야 했다”는 뜻으로 해석했습니다. 확정된 사실로 추가한 정보가 아니라 첨삭 시의 해석입니다. 이동 시간이 더 짧다는 뜻이었다면 이 문장을 다시 바꿔야 합니다.

**We couldn’t hear them at all.**은 옆방의 춤추는 소리가 들리지 않았다는 뜻으로 해석했습니다.

## 기억할 교정

- `Today's weather is a really bad.` → **The weather was really bad today.**: 오늘 있었던 일은 과거형으로, 셀 수 없는 **weather**에는 `a`를 붙이지 않습니다.
- `A rains fall down early morning.` → **It started raining early in the morning.**: 비가 오기 시작하다 = **start raining**.
- `my shoes supposed to get being wet` → **I thought my shoes would get wet**: 젖을 것이라는 예상은 **would get wet**. **be supposed to**는 보통 예정·의무를 나타냅니다.
- `2 station / take off the bus` → **two stops / get off the bus**: 버스 정류장과 버스에서 내리기.
- `unefficient` → **inefficient**: 철자 교정. 이 문맥에서는 **It felt like a waste.** — 아깝게 느껴졌다 — 로 표현했습니다.
- `Hounestly / loose weight` → **Honestly / lose weight**: **loose**는 “헐렁한”이라는 형용사.
- `bad weather made me can't walk` → **The bad weather kept me from walking.**: **keep + 사람 + from + 동사-ing**.
- `we have gone a new place` → **we went to a new study room**: 오늘 다녀온 일은 **went to**로 표현합니다.
- `very silence place inspite of…` → **It was very quiet, even though…**: **quiet**은 형용사, **silence**는 명사. 문장을 연결할 때 **even though + 주어 + 동사**. 명사구 앞에는 **in spite of**도 가능합니다.
- `Unfortunly / white board / pulbic traffic` → **Unfortunately / whiteboard / public transportation**.
- `an hour more earlier` → **an hour earlier**: 비교급 **earlier**에 `more`를 붙이지 않습니다.
- `1 people didn't present` → **One person couldn’t make it.**: 한 명은 **one person**, 참석하지 못하다는 **couldn’t make it**.
- `equiped / compotable` → **equipped / comfortable**: **The room was equipped with a TV.**처럼 쓸 수도 있습니다. 일기에서는 쉽게 **The room had a TV.**로 정리했습니다.

## 통째로 외울 표현

- **I got off after just two stops.** — 겨우 두 정거장 만에 내렸어요.
- **I’m trying to lose weight.** — 살을 빼려고 노력하고 있어요.
- **The bad weather kept me from walking.** — 날씨가 나빠서 걷지 못했어요.
- **We had to look for another place.** — 다른 장소를 찾아야 했어요.
- **One person couldn’t make it today.** — 오늘 한 명이 참석하지 못했어요.
