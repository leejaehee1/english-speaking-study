## 자연스러운 영어 첨삭본

Last weekend, I went to Inje in Gangwon-do. I had made plans to meet up with some alumni from my university at an older colleague's second home. The house sits 450 meters above sea level, and there was a wide, open view right in front of it. The scenery was so beautiful, and the weather was a little chilly.

We hung out on the lawn, which was covered with nice grass. We played with a toy airplane launcher gun—when we pulled the trigger, the airplane flew right into the air. We also put on baseball gloves and played catch together.

Hanging out with my junior colleague's kids made me feel peaceful and relaxed at first. There are three of them: the oldest is 10 years old, the middle one is 7, and the youngest is 5. All three of them are boys, so they were super energetic and cheerful! Especially the youngest one—he was full of energy and very curious. He never stopped moving around and was always asking questions, though he was very cute and fun.

Because of that, my whole weekend just disappeared in the blink of an eye! Still, I was really happy to meet such lovely kids. Next time, though, I don't think I'll go there—I need to rest on the weekend!

## 주요 교정

- **went to Gangwondo Inje → went to Inje in Gangwon-do (또는 Inje, Gangwon-do)** — 영어의 주소·지명 순서는 한국어와 반대로 **작은 지역(인제) → 큰 지역(강원도)** 순으로 씁니다.
- **made a reservation with our colleague in my graduated university → made plans to meet up with some alumni (또는 colleagues) from my university** — 사람과 만날 약속을 잡는 것은 **make plans (to meet)** 이며, 같은 대학교·대학원 동문/선후배는 **alumni**(단수 `alumnus`) 또는 **friends / colleagues from my university** 라고 표현합니다.
- **This house was placed 450 meters above sea level. → The house is located (또는 sits) 450 meters above sea level.** — **450 meters above sea level**(해발 450미터) 표현을 정말 정확하게 잘 쓰셨습니다! 다만 집·건물이 위치해 있을 때는 `placed`(손으로 놓아두다) 대신 **is located** 또는 **sits** 를 씁니다.
- **there were wide view in front the house → there was a wide, open view in front of the house** — `a wide view`는 단수이므로 **there was** 를 쓰고, ‘~ 앞에’는 **in front of ~**(`of` 필수)로 씁니다.
- **the sight was so beautiful and little chill → the scenery was so beautiful, and it was a little chilly** — 자연 풍경·경치는 `sight`보다 **scenery**(또는 **view**)가 자연스럽고, 날씨가 ‘살짝 쌀쌀한’은 형용사 **chilly**(`a little chilly`)를 씁니다.
- **hung out the ground that was coverd with good grass → hung out on the lawn, which was covered with nice grass** — 장소 위에서 시간을 보낼 때는 전치사 **on** 을 쓰며, 철자는 **covered**(`e` 포함)입니다. 잔디가 깔린 마당은 한 단어로 **lawn** 이라고도 부릅니다.
- **We throwed the gun on the airplane toy, when it trigged then airplane started flying away. → We played with a toy airplane launcher gun—when we pulled the trigger, the airplane flew away.** — 두 가지를 기억하세요:
  1. `throw`(던지다)의 과거형은 불규칙 변화인 **threw**(`throw – threw – thrown`)입니다(`throwed` X).
  2. 방아쇠를 당기다는 **pull the trigger** 라고 표현합니다.
- **we throwed baseball's ball against together with globe → we put on baseball gloves and played catch together** — 야구공을 서로 던지고 받으며 노는(캐치볼) 것은 원어민들이 항상 **play catch** 라고 표현합니다! 또한 야구공은 그냥 **a baseball**, 야구 장갑(글러브)의 철자는 **glove**(`v`)입니다(`globe`는 지구본).
- **First one is 10 years old, second one is 7 years old, last one is 5 years old. → The oldest is 10 years old, the middle one is 7, and the youngest is 5.** — 삼 형제 중 첫째·둘째·막내를 말할 때는 **the oldest, the middle one, the youngest** 라고 하면 가장 자연스럽습니다.
- **All the children is male. So they was ... → All three of them are boys, so they were ...** — `children`과 `they`는 복수이므로 동사는 **are / were** 로 맞추고, 남자아이들은 딱딱한 `male` 대신 **boys** 를 씁니다.
- **very curiosity person ... always make a question → a very curious kid ... was always asking questions** — `curiosity`는 명사(‘호기심’)이고 형용사는 **curious**(호기심이 많은)입니다. ‘질문을 하다’는 `make a question`이 아니라 **ask a question / ask questions** 를 씁니다.
- **my last weekend was deleted → my whole weekend just disappeared (또는 flew by)!** — 한국어 유머 표현인 **“주말이 순삭됐다(삭제됐다)”** 를 재치 있게 쓰셨네요! 영어로는 **My weekend just disappeared!** 또는 **My weekend flew by in the blink of an eye!** 라고 하면 똑같은 유머 뉘앙스가 전달됩니다.
- **Next time I didn't go there. I need to relax in weekend. → Next time, I won't go there (또는 I don't think I'll go there). I need to relax on the weekend.** — ‘다음번에는 안 갈 거다’는 미래의 일이므로 과거형 `didn't` 대신 **won't**(`will not`)를 쓰고, ‘주말에’는 **on the weekend** 로 씁니다.

## 의미 해석 메모

- 한국어에서는 친한 후배를 ‘동생(`younger brother`)’, 선배를 ‘형(`older brother / colleague`)’이라고 부르지만, 영어에서 `my younger brother's children`이라고 하면 100% **친남동생의 아이들(조카들, `my nephews`)** 로 이해합니다. 대학교·대학원 후배의 아이들이라면 **my junior colleague's kids**(또는 **my friend's kids**), 친남동생의 아이들이라면 **my younger brother's kids (my three nephews)** 로 구분해 쓰시면 됩니다.
