## 자연스러운 영어 첨삭본

Yesterday, the weather wasn't bad. It was a little chilly, but it was warmer during the day than at other times. I wasn't feeling great because I got drunk with my graduate school friends the day before, so I had a hangover yesterday. Nevertheless, I felt good because I love studying and working on things to improve my skills.

Also, I thought my vibe coding course was going to bring us great results. We made a plan and wrote down the topic, objectives, pain points, and user interfaces in a document. Now, we are thinking about everyday life in more detail for our projects. Once we finalize the idea, we will get ready to develop it. Developing our projects isn't actually difficult. The other members just don't know the exact step-by-step process for building them yet because they have never done this before. So, they need someone to guide them—and that's me! Just kidding.

Honestly, my goal for this week is to speak fluently about food. So, I've been trying to listen to food-related clips and figure out useful expressions about food. Today is the start of the weekend, so I'm going to start talking about food with anyone I meet!

## 주요 교정

- **the weather was not bad and some cold, but daytime was warm better than other times → the weather wasn't bad. It was a little chilly (또는 a bit cold), but it was warmer during the day than at other times.** —
  1. 형용사 앞에서 ‘약간(조금) 추운’은 `some cold` 대신 **a little cold (또는 chilly)** / **a bit cold** 를 씁니다.
  2. `warm`의 비교급은 `warm better`가 아니라 **warmer** 이며, ‘낮에는’은 **during the day** 로 표현합니다.
- **I was bad because previous day I got drunken ... So, I had a hangover yesterday. → I wasn't feeling great because I got drunk ... the day before, so I had a hangover yesterday.** —
  1. 몸 컨디션이 안 좋았을 때는 `I was bad` 대신 **I wasn't feeling well (또는 great)** 라고 합니다.
  2. ‘그 전날’은 정관사 `the`를 넣어 **the day before**(또는 **the previous day**)로 씁니다.
  3. ‘술에 취했다’는 `got drunken`이 아니라 **got drunk** 입니다. 이어진 **`I had a hangover yesterday.`** 는 아주 정확하고 자연스럽게 잘 쓰셨습니다!
- **loved studing and working something to get better my ability → love studying and working on things to improve my skills** —
  1. 철자 교정: `studing` → **studying**(`y` 유지).
  2. 어떤 일·과제를 잡고 작업할 때는 전치사 **on** 을 써서 **working on things** 라고 합니다.
  3. ‘내 능력(실력)을 향상시키다’는 타동사 **improve** 를 써서 **to improve my skills (또는 abilities)** 라고 표현합니다.
- **We mad a planning and wrote a subject, objects, pain points, user interfaces on the document. → We made a plan and wrote down the topic, objectives, pain points, and user interfaces in a document.** —
  1. ‘계획을 세웠다’는 **made a plan** 입니다(`mad` → `made`).
  2. 프로젝트의 ‘목표(목적)’는 `objects`(물체)가 아니라 **objectives**(또는 **goals**)입니다! **`pain points`**(사용자의 불편 지점)와 **`user interfaces`** 는 실무 기획 용어로 아주 훌륭하게 쓰셨습니다.
- **for ours projects more detailed ... Developing ours projects → in more detail for our projects ... Developing our projects** — 명사(`projects`) 앞에서는 `ours`가 아니라 소유격 **our**(**our projects**)를 씁니다. ‘더 자세하게’는 부사구 **in more detail** 로 씁니다.
- **we never have done this. ... It's me. Just joking. → we have never done this before. ... and that's me! Just kidding.** — 현재완료에서 `never`는 `have`와 과거분사(`done`) 사이에 놓습니다(**have never done**). 가볍게 농담임을 밝힐 때는 **Just kidding!** 또는 **I'm just joking!** 이라고 합니다.
- **fluent speaking for food's subject ... listen to food's clip and figure food's expressions out ... I gonna start talking the food → speak fluently about food ... listen to food-related clips and figure out food expressions ... I'm gonna start talking about food** —
  1. 사물·주제(`food`)에는 소유격 `'s`(`food's subject`)를 붙이지 않고 **about food**, **food-related clips**, **food expressions** 처럼 씁니다.
  2. 구동사 **`figure out`**(파악하다·이해하다)을 정말 잘 활용하셨습니다!
  3. 구어체 `gonna` 앞에는 `be`동사(**`I'm gonna`**)가 필요하며, ‘~에 대해 이야기하다’는 전치사 **about** 을 넣어 **talking about food** 라고 합니다.

## 의미 해석 메모

- 바이브 코딩 기획 회의에서 정리하신 4가지 핵심 요소는 영어로 발표할 때도 그대로 쓸 수 있는 실무 표현입니다:
  - **Topic / Subject** (주제) · **Objectives / Goals** (목표) · **Pain Points** (사용자가 겪는 핵심 불편함) · **User Interface (UI)** (사용자 화면 구성)

## 통째로 익힐 Chunk

- **warmer during the day** — 낮 동안에는 더 따뜻한.
- **get drunk / have a hangover** — 술에 취하다 / 숙취가 있다.
- **work on things to improve my skills** — 내 실력을 키우기 위해 무언가를 연구하고 작업하다.
- **write down the topic, objectives, pain points, and user interfaces** — 주제, 목표, 페인 포인트, UI를 문서에 적다.
- **in more detail** — 더 자세하게, 더 구체적으로.
- **finalize the idea** — 아이디어를 최종 정리하다(확정하다).
- **the step-by-step process** — 단계별 과정(절차).
- **That's me! Just kidding.** — 그게 바로 저예요! 농담이에요.
- **figure out useful expressions about food** — 음식에 관한 유용한 표현들을 파악하다(익히다).
- **start talking about food with anyone** — 누구와든 음식 이야기를 시작하다.
