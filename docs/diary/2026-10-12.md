## 교정한 일기

Today was a really good day. I started an English study group today, and the other members seem really nice.

Recently, I haven't been able to focus on work or do anything productive. Last week, I went out drinking on three different days with friends, people from a social club, and fellow students. I felt bad because I had spent too much time doing things that didn't feel worthwhile.

I wanted to do something productive, so I joined this study group last weekend.

Our group leader told us to complete our homework on time every day. The first assignment is to record ourselves reading about a page of text and try to sound as close to a native speaker as possible. The second is to copy a text by hand. We can use our own diary entries or scripts from sources like the BBC or EBS.

We plan to focus on English every day for the next two months and take our English to the next level. That really motivated me.

We've all struggled with learning English before, but now I'm ready to focus on it. My goal is to become more confident speaking English by this winter, especially in everyday situations in Vancouver.
