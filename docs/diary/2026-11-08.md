## 자연스러운 영어 첨삭본

Yesterday, I met up with a younger friend whom I first met through a food-and-drink gathering on Karrot. It had been a long time since we last met because I was so busy last month that I couldn't make plans with him. He had called me about four times to meet on the weekends, but I couldn't make it. Yesterday, we finally caught up on our recent lives together.

Actually, I had mixed feelings at first because he hadn't kept his promise about paying me back. Still, as the older one, I couldn't bring it up first—and besides, I really wanted to see him anyway.

Honestly, he has been going through a really tough situation recently. Both his grandmother and his uncle on his mom's side passed away, and his mom is currently sick with cancer. So he wanted to explain why he hadn't been able to pay me back or tell me about it sooner. I felt a little nervous, but he brought it up first. So I told him, "Don't worry about it at all. Thank you for telling me first."

## 주요 교정

- **I met my younger friend who had met a carrot group that is eating food and drinking meeting. → I met up with a younger friend whom I first met through a food-and-drink gathering on Karrot.** —
  1. 관계절 안에서 ‘그 친구가 모임을 만난’(`who had met a group`) 것이 아니라 ‘내가 당근 모임을 통해 만난 친구’이므로 **whom (또는 who) I met through ~** 로 씁니다.
  2. 당근마켓(Karrot)의 맛집·술자리 모임은 **a food-and-drink gathering (또는 meetup) on Karrot** 이라고 표현하면 자연스럽습니다.
- **we met yesterday for a long time. Because I was very busy last month, so I could't have a reservation. → We met for the first time in a long time. Because I was very busy last month, I couldn't make plans with him.** —
  1. **오랜만에 만나다**: `met for a long time`은 ‘만나서 여러 시간 동안 오래 있었다’는 뜻이 됩니다. ‘오랜만에 만났다’는 **We met for the first time in a long time**(또는 **It had been a long time since we last met**)이라고 합니다.
  2. 친구와 만날 약속을 잡다는 식당 좌석 예약(`reservation`) 대신 **make plans (with him)** 를 씁니다. 또한 영어에서는 한 문장에 `Because`와 `so`를 겹쳐 쓰지 않습니다.
- **talked about recent life together → caught up on our recent lives together (또는 talked about how we've been doing lately)** — 오랜만에 만나 그동안 밀린 근황 이야기를 나누는 것을 원어민들은 구동사 **`catch up`**(과거형 **`caught up`**)으로 표현합니다.
- **he had had a missing appointment about money ... I couldn't tell this by older man. → he hadn't kept his promise about paying me back ... as the older one, I couldn't bring it up first.** —
  1. 돈(변제) 약속을 지키지 못했다는 말은 `missing appointment`(`appointment`는 병원·업무 등 시간 예약) 대신 **he hadn't kept his promise about money (또는 paying me back)** 라고 합니다.
  2. ‘형으로서(연장자 입장에서)’는 전치사 `by` 대신 **as the older one** 을 쓰며, 꺼내기 어려운 화제를 말하다는 11월 5일 수업에서 배운 구동사 **`bring up`**(**I couldn't bring it up first**)을 쓰면 딱 맞습니다!
- **his grand mother died and his mom's brother also died. And His mom is sicking for cancel. → both his grandmother and his uncle on his mom's side passed away, and his mom is sick with cancer (또는 battling cancer).** —
  1. 돌아가셨다는 표현은 `died`보다 정중하고 따뜻한 완곡어법인 **`passed away`** 를 주로 씁니다. ‘외삼촌(`mom's brother`)’은 **his uncle on his mom's side**(또는 **his maternal uncle**)라고 합니다.
  2. **`sicking for cancel` → `sick with cancer` (또는 `battling cancer`)**:
     - `sick`은 형용사이므로 `-ing`를 붙이지 않고 **is sick with + 병명** 으로 씁니다.
     - **철자·발음 주의**: ‘취소하다’는 **cancel**(`/ˈkæn.səl/`, 끝이 `-cel`)이고, 질병인 ‘암’은 **cancer**(`/ˈkæn.sər/`, 끝이 `-cer`)입니다! ‘암 투병 중이다’는 **is battling (또는 fighting) cancer** 라고도 자주 표현합니다.
- **I don't care of it. Thank you for telling me you first. → "Don't worry about it at all. Thank you for telling me first."** —
  1. 상대방을 안심시키며 “그 돈 일은 신경 쓰지 마(괜찮아)”라고 할 때 `I don't care`라고 하면 자칫 “난 상관 안 해(내 알 바 아니야)”처럼 차갑게 들릴 수 있습니다. 이럴 때는 **"Don't worry about it at all."** 또는 **"It's okay, don't worry about it."** 이라고 해야 형으로서의 따뜻한 배려가 온전히 전달됩니다.
  2. “네가 먼저 말해 줘서 고마워”는 `you`를 빼고 **Thank you for telling me first.**(또는 **Thank you for bringing it up first.**)라고 씁니다.

## 의미 해석 메모

- 지난 11월 5일 수업에서 배운 **`bring up`**(화제를 꺼내다)과 10월 13일 영작 숙제에서 배운 **`go through`**(어려운 시기를 겪다)가 오늘 일기 상황에 그대로 쓰였습니다:
  - **As the older one, I couldn't bring it up first.** (형으로서 내가 먼저 그 말을 꺼낼 수 없었다.)
  - **He has been going through a really tough time recently.** (그 동생은 최근에 정말 힘든 시기를 겪고 있었다.)

## 통째로 익힐 Chunk

- **a food-and-drink gathering on Karrot** — 당근(Karrot)의 맛집·술자리 모임.
- **for the first time in a long time** — 오랜만에.
- **catch up on our recent lives** — 서로의 최근 근황 이야기를 나누다.
- **have mixed feelings** — 기분이 복잡미묘하다(마냥 편하지만은 않다).
- **keep one's promise about paying me back** — 돈을 갚기로 한 약속을 지키다.
- **as the older one, I couldn't bring it up first** — 연장자(형)로서 내가 먼저 그 말을 꺼낼 수 없었다.
- **pass away** — 돌아가시다(별세하시다).
- **be sick with cancer / battle cancer** — 암으로 편찮으시다 / 암 투병 중이다 (`cancer` `/ˈkæn.sər/` vs. `cancel` `/ˈkæn.səl/`).
- **Don't worry about it at all.** — 그 일은 전혀 신경 쓰지(걱정하지) 마.
- **Thank you for telling me first.** — 먼저 말해 줘서 고마워.
