## 자연스러운 영어 첨삭본

It was a good day today, even though I drank with my friends from graduate school yesterday. Fortunately, I took Ursa, a medicine that helps my liver and prevents hangovers. Thanks to that, I felt pretty good.

In the morning, I went to Sejong University to teach a course in collaboration with Multicampus. Multicampus is a very famous training center in the IT field. Sejong University is located right near Gunja Station, so it's in a great location for public transportation, and I was able to get there quickly.

Only two students attended the class today. There are only seven students enrolled in this course in total, and yesterday the manager called me and said that only one to three students would show up today. Hearing that made me feel relieved. I had been nervous because it wasn't my own course, so I had to prepare a lot more to teach it. After getting the manager's call, I wasn't nervous anymore and decided to have a few drinks with my friends.

In the end, today's class went really well, and I was able to teach at a comfortable pace with a calm face. Everything worked out great, and I was very satisfied.

## 주요 교정

- **It was good day today, actually I have drunken yesterday with my friends fo the graduate school. → It was a good day today, even though I drank with my friends from graduate school yesterday.** —
  1. 단수 가산명사 `day` 앞에는 부정관사 **a**(`a good day`)를 꼭 씁니다.
  2. `yesterday`는 특정 과거 시점이므로 현재완료(`have + p.p.`) 대신 단순과거 **drank** 를 씁니다.
  3. ‘대학원 친구들’은 전치사 **from** 을 써서 **my friends from graduate school** 이라고 합니다.
- **I had a Woo-lu-sa that is madicine ... against hangover → I took Ursa, a medicine that helps with hangovers** — 약이나 영양제를 ‘먹다(복용하다)’는 동사 **take**(과거형 **took**)를 씁니다. ‘약’의 철자는 **medicine**(`/ˈmed.ɪ.sən/`, `m-e-d-i-c-i-n-e`)입니다.
- **So that, I was not bad. → Thanks to that (또는 So), I felt pretty good.** — 문장 맨 앞에서 ‘그래서’를 뜻할 때는 `So that,`이 아니라 **So,** 또는 ‘그 덕분에’를 뜻하는 **Thanks to that,** 을 씁니다(`so that + 주어 + 동사`는 ‘~하기 위해서’라는 목적을 나타냅니다).
- **went to Sae-jong university colaborated with multicampus ... in IT part → went to Sejong University to teach a course in collaboration with Multicampus ... in the IT field** —
  1. ‘~와 협력하여’는 **in collaboration with ~**(`collaboration` 철자 `l` 두 개)로 표현합니다.
  2. ‘IT 분야에서’는 `part` 대신 **in the IT field**(또는 **in the IT industry**)를 씁니다.
- **is located at Goon-ja station. So that's very good location of transpotation. → is located right near Gunja Station, so it's in a great location for public transportation.** — 지난 일기에서 교정했던 **`is located`** 를 이번 일기에 정확하게 활용하셨네요! ‘교통’의 철자는 `r`이 들어간 **transportation**(`t-r-a-n-s-p-o-r-t-a-t-i-o-n`)입니다.
- **today attendees was just 2 students. This course's total member's number is just 7. → Only two students attended today. There are only seven students in this course in total.** — 복수(`two students`)에는 복수 동사를 쓰며, 명사의 소유격(`'s`)을 연달아 두 번(`course's total member's`) 쓰기보다 **There are only seven students in this course in total** 로 풀어 쓰면 훨씬 자연스럽습니다.
- **the manager called me by phone and said tommorow will be present students's count is one to three → the manager called me and said that only one to three students would attend today** — `call` 자체에 ‘전화로 연락하다’라는 뜻이 들어 있으므로 `by phone`은 생략합니다. ‘내일’의 철자는 **tomorrow**(`m` 1개, `r` 2개)입니다.
- **I'm relaxed ... I'm not nervous and I decided to have a alchol a little. → I felt relieved (또는 relaxed) ... I wasn't nervous anymore and decided to have a few drinks (또는 drink a little).** — 어제 매니저의 전화를 받고 안심했던 과거 시점이므로 과거형(**felt relieved / wasn't nervous**)으로 시제를 일치시킵니다. 또한 ‘술·알코올’의 철자는 **alcohol**(`/ˈæl.kə.hɔːl/`)이며 셀 수 없는 명사이므로 `a`를 붙이지 않고, 일상 회화에서는 **have a few drinks**(술을 한두 잔 마시다)라고 표현합니다.
- **Finally, today was so good result. And I can teach so common face. → In the end, today's class went really well, and I was able to teach at a comfortable pace (또는 with a calm face).** —
  1. ‘결국(결과적으로) 오늘 수업이 아주 잘 끝났다’는 **In the end, today's class went really well.** 이라고 합니다.
  2. `common face`는 문맥상 **‘편안한 속도/페이스로(`at a comfortable pace`)’** 또는 **‘차분하고 평온한 표정으로(`with a calm face`)’** 를 뜻하는 것으로 보여 두 표현을 모두 자연스럽게 살려 첨삭했습니다(`pace` `/peɪs/`: 속도·보조 vs. `face` `/feɪs/`: 얼굴·표정).
